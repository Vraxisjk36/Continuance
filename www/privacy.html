Privacy Policy

Continuance: Psychological RPG

Effective: 1 November 2026 · Developer: vraxisDev

vraxisDev respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled in Continuance: Psychological RPG, including playtesting and the planned commercial release.

1. Gameplay and local data

The game saves information such as trial progress, puzzle results, story decisions, unlocked endings and preferences locally on your device. This supports saving and resuming play. Local storage alone does not transmit that information to vraxisDev. Device backup features may separately back up app data depending on your settings.

2. Playtesting and feedback

The planned playtest provides access to the 12 main trials without payment. If you voluntarily email us feedback or a bug report, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and any attached screenshots or technical details. We use it to respond and improve the game.

3. Planned purchases

In the planned official release, Trials 1–4 will be free and Trials 5–12 will be available through a one-time in-app purchase using Google Play Billing. Google processes payments under its own policies. vraxisDev will not directly collect your full payment-card details. The game may handle purchase status and transaction identifiers to unlock or restore content. This feature is planned and may not be active during playtesting.

4. Third-party services

Google Play provides app distribution and may independently process information under Google's Privacy Policy. If analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud saves or other third-party SDKs are added, this policy will be revised to reflect their actual practices before release.

5. Use and sharing

Information is used to maintain gameplay, support purchases when enabled, answer support requests, resolve bugs and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers as needed for those purposes or disclosed when legally required.

6. Retention, security and deletion

Local progress generally remains until app data is cleared or the game is uninstalled, subject to device backup and restore behavior. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary for support and legal purposes. To request access to or deletion of information held directly by vraxisDev, contact us at the address below. No electronic storage method is completely secure.

7. Children and age suitability

Continuance contains psychological suspense and potentially mature narrative themes and is not specifically designed for young children. The applicable Google Play age rating will be determined through its content rating process. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information to us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes, particularly when paid content or new online services launch. The effective date above will be revised when changes are published, with additional notice or consent where required by law.
